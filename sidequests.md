# Sidequests

## Eevee collection
Acquire one of every Eeveelution to unlock Eeveeite. The roster is intended to cover all 19 types including Acid and a dedicated Normal evolution. The same stone is proposed to Mega Evolve every Eeveelution; all nineteen Mega forms have established stats and abilities. Heritage Revival is the proposed quest sponsor. Simultaneous ownership rather than Pokedex registration is a proposed requirement, not finalized. No mandatory sponsorship; sponsors can make breeding and item acquisition easier. See [Pokemon](pokemon.md).

## Abandoned Eeveelutions

Proposed mini-sidequest linked to privileged, type-focused Safari habitats with increased hidden ability encounter rates. These areas also serve as environmental evolution catalysts comparable to Moss Rock and Ice Rock: high-friendship Eevee can evolve on leveling up in the corresponding habitat. The six planned habitats and their corresponding species are listed in [Safari habitats](progression-and-player-access.md#specialized-safari-habitats). Existing evolution routes remain available.

An NPC in each relevant habitat complains that their beautiful Eevee evolved into something disgusting while training against local wild Pokemon, indirectly revealing the environmental evolution trigger. After the player obtains Eeveeite, returning to the habitat may reveal a unique wild encounter with the corresponding Eeveelution, abandoned by its former owner. Guaranteed shiny encounters are proposed: the former owner's praise of Eevee's beauty refers to its shiny coloration, highlighting conditional affection and manufactured desirability. Exact encounter levels, abilities, retry rules and dialogue remain TBD. These encounters are not required for the Eeveeite collection quest, and ordinary evolution routes must remain accessible beforehand.

## Historical badge collection

Eight original metal/alloy gym badges survive as scarce historical collectibles; see [badge names](factions-groups-gyms.md#historical-and-corporate-badges). An incidental NPC comment mentions that history buffs and collectors remember the old badges, but many have been lost or deteriorated through exposure to the elements.

Each badge is a unique quest item. Returning it to the corresponding displaced leader while rejecting the relevant corporate sponsorship is part of persuading that leader to petition the Elite Four for reinstatement. A successful petition removes the badge from inventory. Alternatively, surrender it to the corporation that displaced the leader for a suggested 10,000 money reward; the corporation promptly disposes of the symbol of the old League. Retaining badges can satisfy prerequisites for certain legendary encounters, such as the Regis; specific badge-to-legendary assignments TBD.

After the League, supported former leaders are reinstated and their historical badge can be earned again by defeating their revamped gym challenge. All eight gyms reopen with revised trainers and leaders whether restored or incumbent. Legendary access may therefore be delayed by a petition until the badge is re-earned. Exact petition eligibility, sold-badge recovery policy, legendary check timing, and interaction with the sixth badge used as fugitive evidence TBD.

## Eden investigations
### The Pikachu that never evolves
NPCs repeat Eden's claim that Plusle and Minun are male/female first-generation Pikachu variants. Trainers are blamed for failed evolution. Investigate daycare egg swapping, inconsistent records, staged demonstrations or misleading advertisements. Actual evolution mechanics do not change. Outcome and rewards TBD.

### The rarest fish
Eden captures Magikarp to control supply. Proposed clues: formerly abundant public waterways become scarce, paid reserves have them, an elder recalls historic abundance, a hidden pond or holding facility reveals the truth. A salesman may charge an absurd price (draft 50,000 versus classic 500). Investigate whether and how the stock is released. Outcome/rewards TBD; this arc may be main story instead.

## Legendary exploration chains
The Swords of Justice chain includes two mutually exclusive favorable resolutions determined by the pre-Gym-6 fugitive decision (see [narrative outline](narrative-outline.md#pre-gym-6-road-closure--fugitive-former-gym-leader)). All three Swords of Justice encounters are accessible only beyond the sixth Gym's city, after the fugitive decision. Virizion requires six distinct friendship-dependent evolved species registered as caught in the Pokedex, not merely seen or currently owned, representing a full team. Completing the Eevee collection quest satisfies this requirement; exact qualifying species list TBD. Keldeo requires successful persuasion of at least two of the three Swords of Justice before the Mewtwo gauntlet. If no legendary quests are completed, Keldeo appears as an opponent. With the mutually exclusive pair, persuading Virizion is necessary to qualify for Keldeo. Exact encounter and capture rules TBD.

Hunting legendary Pokemon and preventing their corruption or recruitment by Mewtwo is gated by puzzles and exploration chains. Mew warns the player of the crisis. Mewtwo engineers corruption of Solarceny and conditions that break Celebi's spirit. Candidate clues can intersect with corporate investigations, gym access and criminal smuggling routes. Each chain must involve more than finding and capturing: gathering environmental clues, NPC testimony, general Pokemon knowledge, and satisfying meaningful conditions (e.g. persuading a legendary in a debate against Mewtwo). Successful capture, dissuasion or other resolution removes that legendary from its gauntlet group; fully resolved groups are skipped. Persistent resolution flags are separate from gauntlet attempt flags, which reset on wipe. Exact roster, puzzles, locations, debate mechanics, capture gating, and whether failed debates can be retried TBD. See [narrative outline](narrative-outline.md).

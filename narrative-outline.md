# Narrative outline

## Premise
Pokemon Patina is the most unoriginal Pokemon game you have ever played. Witness everything you have already seen and done, repackaged and sold to you again as a "Remaster."

Gen IX, all of its Pokemon and mechanics, back ported to a GBA-like experience.

Pokemon have been hyper commodified into industrial beasts, work horses, mascots, tools, and status symbols. Almost no one in this region remembers the inherent joy of their first capture or the first evolution. Even trainers who purport to be lovers of their teams only talk about them in terms of competitive viability and marketability.

The environment of this Pokemon region is suffering from worsening industrial pollution, which has caused the emergence of regional evolutions that the corporations point to as proof that they are benefiting Pokemon, not harming them.

The eight corporations each sponsor a gym and have co-opted the purpose of Pokemon gyms into little more than advertising grounds.

As the player adventures, they will have the option to accept sponsorship from the corporations for various boons like increased prize money, faster egg hatching, and preferential safari access. Refusing these opportunities will be part of a harder, "true" path.

## Narrative principles
Industry need not be inherently evil; exploitation and commodification are the targets. Natural Pokemon should exist independently of economic usefulness. Acid is a natural as well as industrial element, not an evil type. Make corporate products appealing enough that the player experiences the tension rather than receiving a lecture. Ordinary people and Pokemon retain agency and value. Corporate propaganda is unreliable; established Pokemon mechanics remain consistent. Some acid types are a result of environmental damage, some would exist regardless of human interference.

## Four intersecting plots
1. League: defeat eight gyms, challenge Elite Four and become Champion. The conventional journey is a complete primary progression path.
2. Corporations: uncover the truth behind the eight rival industries and decide whether to accept or reject sponsorships. Choices lead to three distinct endings; exact thresholds and consequences TBD. Sponsorship remains optional with real benefits and no compensation for refusal except pride.
3. Legendary crisis: Mew warns the player that environmental destruction and Pokemon exploitation recreate the conditions behind Mewtwo's rage. Mewtwo recruits legendary Pokemon and turns them against humanity, engineers corruption of Solarceny, the unique Dark/Electric solar legendary, and places Celebi in conditions that break its spirit (Grass/Acid corrupted form). Mewtwo is a post-Elite Four antagonist. Legendary hunts and efforts to prevent corruption/recruitment are unlocked through puzzles and exploration chains. The four plots intersect; exact timing, encounter rules and resolution TBD.
4. Criminal organization: a Team Rocket-like organization exploits environmental conditions and commercial culture through bootleg merchandise, Pokemon misinformation, smuggling and related schemes. Team Gild. Leadership, corporate relationships and resolution TBD.

Mewtwo exploits real human wrongdoing as a means of turning legendary Pokemon against humanity, rather than directly being the source of any damage/catastrophe directly.
Post-League Mewtwo conflict reflects legendary quest outcomes through the Legendary Gauntlet. Do not require sponsorship to access core story.

## Pre-Gym-6 road closure — fugitive former Gym Leader

A criminal act involving Team Gild closes the roads to the sixth Gym's city for investigation. Progress requires entering Second Chance Reserve and seeking a local expert: its most cynical trainer, a former Gym Leader forced out after refusing sponsorship because he foresaw the commercialization of competitive breeding and mass abandonment. He briefly joined Team Gild believing it could overthrow corporate power, participated in the criminal act, and hides in the reserve because it is the last place acquaintances would expect him to be. Exact identity, former gym assignment, crime and investigation details TBD.

The former sixth Gym Leader's historical gym badge is used as evidence that he was found or fled the region. The player must either turn him in or submit false evidence that he fled the region. Both choices reopen the roads and allow normal League progression. Turning him in is a prerequisite for sponsorship after defeating Gym 6; submitting false evidence bars sponsorship after that milestone. Handling of previously accepted sponsorships and precise enforcement timing TBD. No sponsorship is required for core progression.

This decision also splits the Swords of Justice: one member rejects Mewtwo if the player turns the fugitive in, while a second rejects the player; the opposite occurs if the player submits false evidence. The two members' favorable resolutions are mutually exclusive within a playthrough. All three Swords of Justice encounters are gated to areas beyond the sixth Gym's city, after the fugitive decision. Virizion independently requires six distinct friendship-dependent evolved species registered as caught in the Pokedex (not merely seen), symbolizing a full team; completing the Eevee collection quest satisfies this threshold. Keldeo requires successful persuasion of at least two of the three Swords before the Mewtwo gauntlet; without legendary quest completion Keldeo is an opponent. The identities of the two opposed members, encounter conditions and capture rules TBD. The rejected branch feeds into the legendary recruitment/gauntlet system; exact implementation TBD.

## Historical League restoration

Corporate gyms award commercial-virtue badges that replaced the original metal/alloy badges. Eight surviving historical badges form a collection quest; returning a badge to its former leader and rejecting sponsorship can support a petition for reinstatement, while selling it to the corresponding corporation yields money and the corporation disposes of it. Certain legendary encounters require historical badges. The Elite Four reinstates supported former leaders in the postgame, and defeating them in revamped gym challenges awards their badge again. All gyms reopen regardless of restoration outcomes. See [sidequests](sidequests.md#historical-badge-collection) and [gyms](factions-groups-gyms.md#postgame-gym-challenges).

## Vitriol — environmental climax
Vitriol is an original mono-Acid legendary with a 600 BST target. Its possible affiliation with the legendary beasts, Swords of Justice or weather legendary group remains undecided. The weather-group concept places Acid between Groudon's land and Kyogre's water; exact mythology and mechanics TBD. A culminating force in the environmental destruction plotline. Team Gild seeks to capture and exploit it to induce targeted ecological collapse, creating desperate victims at will for commercial/criminal exploitation. This intersects the criminal and legendary plots; exact powers, motivations, encounter timing and relationship to Mewtwo are TBD. Candidate Yellow Orb (Yellow Gem) unlocks Vitriol's Primal Reversion using the existing pokeemerald-expansion mechanic. Team Gild pursues the artifact even if the player captures Vitriol first, preserving a quest objective after capture. Exact acquisition sequence and villain contingency remain open. Primal Vitriol establishes persistent Acid Rain that blocks ordinary weather replacement but can be overridden by other primordial weather abilities. Its ability dissolves existing entry hazards on both sides on activation; switching and subsequent hazard placement remain unrestricted. See [weather mechanics](acid-type.md#primal-acid-rain).

Heliosnore is a distinct Normal/Electric species derived from a divergent Snorlax lineage. Solarceny is its unique Dark/Electric corrupted form, central to Mewtwo's plot. Its appearance shifts from relaxed/sleeping to menacing, forward-hunched and predatory. Exact corruption and restoration mechanics remain TBD.

## Legendary Gauntlet
Post-Elite Four, Mewtwo is reached through a gym-like dungeon with consecutive battles against recruited legendary groups (e.g. birds and beasts), using only unresolved/recruited members. If Articuno and Moltres have been successfully dealt with, the birds encounter contains only Zapdos. If all members are resolved, skip that encounter entirely. Complete all remaining battles and Mewtwo in one attempt; a wipe resets all gauntlet battles on return. No Pokemon Center recovery between encounters; voluntary departure resets the run. Encounter order and exact rosters remain TBD. Legendary chains require gathering environmental and NPC clues, general Pokemon knowledge, and satisfying conditions including potentially persuading the legendary in a debate against Mewtwo; merely locating and capturing is insufficient as the entire quest design. See sidequests.md.

## Eden plot
Eden falsely markets Plusle/Minun as sex variants of first-generation Pikachu and blames trainers for failed evolution. It manipulates daycare eggs to sustain the lie. Its larger project is to remove Magikarp from circulation and manufacture rarity. See [factions](factions-groups-gyms.md) and [sidequests](sidequests.md).

# Narrative outline

## Established premise
Pokemon Patina is an original native GBA ROM hack. Use established community mechanics and backported species where practical; pokeemerald-expansion is the proposed foundation. Industrialism is literal (environmental destruction and exploitation of Pokemon) and metatextual (nostalgia, remakes and remasters extracting value from things no longer appreciated for inherent beauty). Eight rival industries sponsor the region's gyms and offer genuinely useful optional sponsorship to the player. Refusal has no compensating reward and is not intended to prevent normal progression.

## Narrative principles
Industry need not be inherently evil; exploitation and commodification are the targets. Natural Pokemon should exist independently of economic usefulness. Acid is a natural as well as industrial element, not an evil type. Make corporate products appealing enough that the player experiences the tension rather than receiving a lecture. Ordinary people and Pokemon retain agency and value. Corporate propaganda is unreliable; established Pokemon mechanics remain consistent.

## Proposed progression scaffold (not an approved plot)
1. Opening: familiar trainer journey, catching and research; introduce sponsorship as a tempting practical choice.
2. Early gyms: encounter contrasting corporate philosophies, useful services and subtle ecological changes. NPC rumors introduce Eden's Plusle/Minun falsehood.
3. Midgame: show visible habitat loss, contested resources and conflicts between businesses. Evidence of daycare egg swaps and artificial scarcity emerges. Player can investigate without requiring refusal of sponsorship.
4. Late game: Eden's Magikarp capture program becomes visible through public waterways, paid reserves and holding facilities; corporate rivalries expose competing incentives. Heritage's Eevee collection campaign exemplifies nostalgia and mascot favoritism.
5. League/endgame: resolution and consequences remain TBD. Do not assume a single villain, mandatory anti-corporate revolution or an ending determined by sponsorship. Consider an ordinary League climax with localized resolutions.

## Established Eden plot
Eden falsely markets Plusle/Minun as sex variants of first-generation Pikachu and blames trainers for failed evolution. It manipulates daycare eggs to sustain the lie. Its larger project is to remove Magikarp from circulation and manufacture rarity. See [factions](factions-groups-gyms.md) and [sidequests](sidequests.md).

## Open decisions
Region/town names, geography, protagonist, rival, professor, gym order, League structure, antagonist(s), final conflict, endings, whether a traditional evil team exists, and the exact place of Eden and Heritage arcs in main versus optional story.

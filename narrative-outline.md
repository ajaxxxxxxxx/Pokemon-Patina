# Narrative outline

## Premise
Pokemon Patina is the most unoriginal Pokemon game you have ever played. Witness everything you have already seen and done, repackaged and sold to you again as a "Remaster."

Gen IX, all of its Pokemon and mechanics, back ported to a GBA-like experience.

Pokemon have been hyper commodified into industrial beasts, work horses, mascots, tools, and status symbols. Almost no one in this region remembers the inherent joy of their first capture or the first evolution. Even trainers who purport to be lovers of their teams only talk about them in terms of competitive viability and marketability.

The environment of this Pokemon region is suffering from worsening industrial pollution, which has caused the emergence of regional evolutions that the corporations point to as proof that they are benefiting Pokemon, not harming them.

The eight corporations each sponsor a gym and have co-opted the purpose of Pokemon gyms into little more than advertising grounds.

As the player adventures, they will have the option to accept sponsorship from the corporations for various boons like increased prize money, faster egg hatching, and preferential safari access. Refusing these opportunities will be part of a harder, "true" path.

## Narrative principles
Industry need not be inherently evil; exploitation and commodification are the targets. Natural Pokemon should exist independently of economic usefulness. Acid is a natural as well as industrial element, not an evil type. Make corporate products appealing enough that the player experiences the tension rather than receiving a lecture. Ordinary people and Pokemon retain agency and value. Corporate propaganda is unreliable; established Pokemon mechanics remain consistent. Some acid types are a result of environmental damage, some would exist regardless of human interference.

## Four intersecting plots
1. League: defeat eight gyms, challenge Elite Four and become Champion. The conventional journey is a complete primary progression path.
2. Corporations: uncover the truth behind the eight rival industries and decide whether to accept or reject sponsorships. Choices lead to three distinct endings; exact thresholds and consequences TBD. Sponsorship remains optional with real benefits and no compensation for refusal except pride.
3. Legendary crisis: Mew warns the player that environmental destruction and Pokemon exploitation recreate the conditions behind Mewtwo's rage. Mewtwo recruits legendary Pokemon and turns them against humanity, engineers corruption of the Dark/Electric solar Pokemon, and places Celebi in conditions that break its spirit (Grass/Acid corrupted form). Mewtwo is a post-Elite Four antagonist. Legendary hunts and efforts to prevent corruption/recruitment are unlocked through puzzles and exploration chains. The four plots intersect; exact timing, encounter rules and resolution TBD.
4. Criminal organization: a Team Rocket-like organization exploits environmental conditions and commercial culture through bootleg merchandise, Pokemon misinformation, smuggling and related schemes. Team Gold. Leadership, corporate relationships and resolution TBD.

Mewtwo exploits real human wrongdoing as a means of turning legendary Pokemon against humanity, rather than directly being the source of any damage/catastrophe directly.
Post-League Mewtwo conflict reflects legendary quest outcomes through the Legendary Gauntlet. Do not require sponsorship to access core story.

## Mono-Acid legendary — environmental climax concept
New original mono-Acid legendary with 600 BST. A culminating force in the environmental destruction plotline. Team Gold seeks to capture and exploit it to induce targeted ecological collapse, creating desperate victims at will for commercial/criminal exploitation. This intersects the criminal and legendary plots; exact powers, motivations, encounter timing and relationship to Mewtwo are TBD.

## Legendary Gauntlet
Post-Elite Four, Mewtwo is reached through a gym-like dungeon with consecutive battles against recruited legendary groups (e.g. birds and beasts), using only unresolved/recruited members. If Articuno and Moltres have been successfully dealt with, the birds encounter contains only Zapdos. If all members are resolved, skip that encounter entirely. Complete all remaining battles and Mewtwo in one attempt; a wipe resets all gauntlet battles on return. No Pokemon Center recovery between encounters; voluntary departure resets the run. Encounter order and exact rosters remain TBD. Legendary chains require gathering environmental and NPC clues, general Pokemon knowledge, and satisfying conditions including potentially persuading the legendary in a debate against Mewtwo; merely locating and capturing is insufficient as the entire quest design. See sidequests.md.

## Eden plot
Eden falsely markets Plusle/Minun as sex variants of first-generation Pikachu and blames trainers for failed evolution. It manipulates daycare eggs to sustain the lie. Its larger project is to remove Magikarp from circulation and manufacture rarity. See [factions](factions-groups-gyms.md) and [sidequests](sidequests.md).

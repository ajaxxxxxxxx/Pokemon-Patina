# Sidequests

## Eevee collection
Acquire one of every Eeveelution to unlock Eeveeite. The roster is intended to cover all 19 types including Acid and a dedicated Normal evolution. The same stone is proposed to Mega Evolve every Eeveelution; the full Mega roster remains under exploration. Heritage Revival is the proposed quest sponsor. Simultaneous ownership rather than Pokedex registration is a proposed requirement, not finalized. No mandatory sponsorship; sponsors can make breeding and item acquisition easier. See [Pokemon](pokemon.md).

## Eden investigations
### The Pikachu that never evolves
NPCs repeat Eden's claim that Plusle and Minun are male/female first-generation Pikachu variants. Trainers are blamed for failed evolution. Investigate daycare egg swapping, inconsistent records, staged demonstrations or misleading advertisements. Actual evolution mechanics do not change. Outcome and rewards TBD.

### The rarest fish
Eden captures Magikarp to control supply. Proposed clues: formerly abundant public waterways become scarce, paid reserves have them, an elder recalls historic abundance, a hidden pond or holding facility reveals the truth. A salesman may charge an absurd price (draft 50,000 versus classic 500). Investigate whether and how the stock is released. Outcome/rewards TBD; this arc may be main story instead.

## Legendary exploration chains
Hunting legendary Pokemon and preventing their corruption or recruitment by Mewtwo is gated by puzzles and exploration chains. Mew warns the player of the crisis. Mewtwo engineers corruption of Solarceny and conditions that break Celebi's spirit. Candidate clues can intersect with corporate investigations, gym access and criminal smuggling routes. Each chain must involve more than finding and capturing: gathering environmental clues, NPC testimony, general Pokemon knowledge, and satisfying meaningful conditions (e.g. persuading a legendary in a debate against Mewtwo). Successful capture, dissuasion or other resolution removes that legendary from its gauntlet group; fully resolved groups are skipped. Persistent resolution flags are separate from gauntlet attempt flags, which reset on wipe. Exact roster, puzzles, locations, debate mechanics, capture gating, and whether failed debates can be retried TBD. See [narrative outline](narrative-outline.md).
